What is FISETIN?

Fisetin is a naturally occurring flavonoid, a plant compound found in foods such as strawberries, apples, onions and cucumbers. It belongs to the flavonol group of plant compounds. Read the ingredient review.

As we age, senescent or “zombie” cells build up in the body. These cells have stopped dividing but remain active. Scientists study cellular senescence as one of the biological processes involved in aging.

Fisetin is studied for its senolytic activity: its interaction with senescent cells. Research has examined these effects in cells, human tissue samples and animals. Read the cellular-senescence study.

Fisetin is also studied for its antioxidant activity and cellular responses to oxidative stress. These research areas help explain the interest in fisetin and healthy aging. Read the antioxidant study.

MASI Premium Fisetin supports a daily healthy-aging routine. Each capsule contains 250 mg of pure Fisetin. Each jar contains 60 vegan capsules. It is made in Germany with German raw materials and third-party tested in Switzerland for purity.

Ingredient research includes different doses, formulations and study designs. Human studies are still developing. Laboratory findings do not establish the same results for a daily supplement routine.

Fisetin Benefits

Fisetin research focuses on cellular senescence, antioxidant activity and the cellular pathways involved in aging.

Cellular renewal and healthy aging

Scientists have studied how fisetin interacts with senescent cells. A 2018 study examined cell cultures, human tissue samples and mice. It helped establish the basis for further human research. Read the study.

Antioxidant activity

Laboratory studies examine how fisetin interacts with reactive molecules and oxidative stress. Researchers also study cellular antioxidant pathways, including Nrf2. Read the laboratory study · Read the cell study.

Healthy brain aging

Fisetin research includes cellular pathways relevant to healthy brain aging. This is an area of ingredient research, rather than a promise of changes in memory or mental performance. Read the research review.

A consistent daily routine

One capsule provides 250 mg of Fisetin. Two capsules provide 500 mg. The MASI directions are one capsule daily for ages 40–50, and two capsules daily for ages 50 and above. Each jar contains 60 vegan capsules.
